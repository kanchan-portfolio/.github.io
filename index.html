About Kanchan

Obsessed With Light, Color & Human Emotion

I am a photographer and cinematographer driven by the relentless pursuit of honest visual storytelling. My style balances raw documentary energy with meticulous cinematic lighting.

With years behind the lens, I partner with forward-thinking brands, musicians, and creators to transform concepts into unforgettable imagery. From remote mountain expeditions to controlled studio stages, every frame is crafted with intention.
